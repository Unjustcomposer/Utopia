# Letter of Intent: Design Partnership

**Date:** July 24, 2026
**To:** The Founders, NexusAI Inc.
**From:** VP of Supply Chain Analytics, NorthStar Retail Group

**Subject: Non-Binding Letter of Intent for Zero-Cost Proof of Concept (PoC)**

To the NexusAI Team:

This Letter of Intent ("LOI") sets forth the preliminary understanding between NorthStar Retail Group ("NorthStar") and NexusAI Inc. ("NexusAI") regarding a proof-of-concept deployment of the NexusAI Supply Chain Stress Tester.

## 1. Pilot Scope & Data
Unlike traditional enterprise deployments requiring live ERP integration, this PoC will be executed entirely offline. NorthStar will provide a static, anonymized CSV export of historical inventory levels, lead times, and SKU sales velocities covering a 24-month period (Jan 2024 - Dec 2025). 

NexusAI will ingest this CSV data to initialize its differentiable graph engine and backtest simulated macro shocks (e.g., Q3 2024 shipping delays) against our actual historical performance.

## 2. Commercial Terms
This is a **zero-cost** Design Partnership. 
- NexusAI waives all software licensing and implementation fees for the duration of the 30-day PoC.
- In exchange, NorthStar agrees to provide weekly feedback sessions with our supply chain analytics team and, upon successful backtesting, agrees to serve as a referenceable case study for NexusAI's upcoming seed round.

## 3. Success Criteria
To validate the technology, NexusAI must:
1. Successfully ingest the 100,000-row static CSV dataset within 24 hours of receipt.
2. Generate gradient-optimized buffer recommendations that outperform NorthStar's historical heuristic buffers by at least 5% in simulated holding costs without increasing stockouts.

## 4. Next Steps
Upon execution of this LOI and a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), NorthStar will securely transfer the anonymized CSV extract to NexusAI via an encrypted S3 bucket.

We are excited to test your differentiable architecture against our historical data.
